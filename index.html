Building Africa's Premier Cybersecurity Ecosystem

CyberShe was born from a clear and present need: to create a dedicated, collaborative space where women in cybersecurity can connect, learn, and build together. We saw brilliant individuals working in silos, facing the same challenges alone. The goal was to build a "tribe"—a professional ecosystem that fosters collective defense, shared knowledge, and African-led solutions.

The Challenge We Address
The cybersecurity landscape in Africa is growing rapidly, but the talent pool remains under-leveraged. Many women in the field lack access to a strong professional network, mentorship, and opportunities to collaborate on real-world problems. This leads to isolation and hinders the development of a robust, Pan-African security community.

Our Ecosystem Approach
CyberShe is the solution. We are not just a community; we are an interconnected ecosystem where members actively co-create. We move beyond networking to *collaboration*. Our members don't just share job posts; they share threat intelligence, audit each other's systems, and build security solutions tailored to Africa's unique infrastructure. Our mantra, "Secure by Design, Led by Women," is our guiding principle.

What We Do Together
Our ecosystem is activated through a variety of virtual-first activities:

  • Cyber & Coffee Mornings: Informal, 35-minute sessions to discuss the latest threats and trends.
  • Threat of the Week: A weekly asynchronous post to build collective intelligence.
  • 30-Minute Security Audits: Hands-on sessions where we collaboratively build security checklists.
  • Reading Circles: Deep dives into articles and reports to foster critical thinking.
  • Regional Threat Mapping: Leveraging our distributed team to understand cyber threats across Kenya and Africa.
  • Spotlight Series: Celebrating the journeys and expertise of our members.
  • "Ask a Security Pro" Office Hours: Providing direct support for career and technical questions.

The Invitation
CyberShe is a movement of doers. We are the women building, breaking, and securing Africa's digital infrastructure. This is not about inclusion—it's about execution. Join us. Let's build the future of cybersecurity in Africa, together.

Phase 2: Launch & Ignition

  • Official Public Launch – January, 2026.
  • First Member Cohort Onboarding.
