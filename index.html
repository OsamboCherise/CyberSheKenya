Founder's Story: From a Spark to a Movement

My name is Cherise Fasey Osambo, and if you were to ask me when CyberShe began, I'd tell you it started in those quiet moments of noticing a gap that shouldn't exist—between brilliant young women and the tech world they deserved to shape.

Chapter 1: The Two Realities
My entry into tech was a dance between awe and isolation. While witnessing Africa's digital revolution, I sat with brilliant young women who saw tech as a distant, locked gate. Their creativity was boundless, yet a persistent whisper told them tech was "not for them." The confidence gap was real; access was unequal. A new narrative was needed—one to be lived, not just heard.

Chapter 2: The Lessons in the Journey
Through African Girls Can Code, I saw proof: give girls tools, and they create. But after bootcamps, momentum faded. Where was the sustained community? As a Siemens EmpowerHer mentor, I witnessed the magic of shared experience—how encouragement turns doubt into determination. Yet even here, brilliant individuals worked in silos. The core issue wasn't talent scarcity; it was the lack of intentional, collaborative spaces designed by and for women.

Chapter 3: The Birth of a Belief
A conviction took root: When women co-create, innovation thrives, scales, and heals. Building a secure future became our perfect metaphor. To secure our digital world, we need diverse builders. Women don't just need a seat at the table—we need our own tribe, our own forge. Thus, CyberShe was born—not as another program, but as an ecosystem.

Chapter 4: What CyberShe Is
This is not just a tech club. It is a declaration:

  • The "Night of Code" where laughter and keystrokes build prototypes for local clinics
  • The "HackSheThon" where young women from marginalized communities stand as architects of the future
  • Traveling workshops showing girls their digital footprint can be leadership, not just consumption
  • Panel discussions where seasoned CTOs pass microphones to first-year students
Our mantra, "Secure by Design, Led by Women," beats at this mission's heart. We're building Africa's premier collaborative ecosystem where tech knowledge is our foundation, and community is our superpower.

Chapter 5: The Invitation
CyberShe is my commitment to replacing "I can't" with "We will." This story has just begun. The next chapters await the names, ideas, and triumphs of every woman who joins this tribe. This is more than my story—this is the beginning of ours.

Join us. Let's build.

With unwavering belief,
Cherise Fasey Osambo
Founder, CyberShe
